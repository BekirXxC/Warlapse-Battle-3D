A supported installation of Warlapse for Windows / Steam is required; the game is not included. The installer makes a separate game copy; saves and settings remain shared.

[h2]What the separate 3D mod adds[/h2]
- A 3D battlefield, free camera and soldier camera for offline battles.
- WASD movement of your selected soldier and G / Esc to return to 2D.
- Era-based soldiers and vehicles, vegetation, sunlight, shadows and weather.
- Optional enhanced graphics with F8.
Combat and damage still use the game's unit-level simulation. These features are in the GitHub mod, not in this Workshop notice.

After deployment in an offline battle, press G for 3D and F for soldier view. The real mod starts in English; use TR / ENG in its main-menu panel or 3D HUD. Hold Ctrl to click HUD buttons. Your language selection is saved.
