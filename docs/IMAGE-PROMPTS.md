# Görsel üretim kayıtları

Araç: yerleşik ImageGen (CLI/API kullanılmadı).

## logo.png

Kullanıcı geri bildirimi üzerine sadeleştirilen son logo. Düz beyaz W, küçük turkuaz ayrıntı, koyu zemin. Önceki süslü logo dağıtıma alınmadı.

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: minimal square game-mod identity, opaque dark background.
Primary request: A VERY simple pristine flat logo for Warlapse Battle 3D. Centered small geometric white W monogram, one small muted teal square above the right terminal. Under the W, two clean lines of understated modern sans serif text exactly "WARLAPSE" then smaller "BATTLE 3D" with wide spacing. Large empty margins, perfectly centered, design occupies middle 60% only. Solid uniform dark charcoal green background (#16221e), opaque, no texture. W shape formed from clean solid white angular bars, absolutely crisp clean typography and edges. No speckles, distress or dirt. No other objects or words. No shield, swords, armor, landscape, metallic effect, gradient, light effect, shadow, border, gloss or bevel. Very restrained simple professional indie app branding. One finished square image.
```

## workshop-cover.png

Yapay zekâ ile üretilmiş tanıtım illüstrasyonu; oyun ekran görüntüsü değildir. Referans: modun 7.6.0 Malazgirt asker kamerası ekran görüntüsü ve sade logo. Görsel üzerinde PROMOTIONAL ART etiketi bulunur.

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: wide Steam Workshop promotional cover, landscape 16:9.
Input images: Image 1 real gameplay screenshot, supporting scene reference. Image 2 approved minimal white W wordmark, brand identity reference.
Primary request: Design a genuinely beautiful restrained premium indie strategy-mod cover. Use the REAL faceted low-poly style of the gameplay screenshot: modest geometric medieval soldiers standing in a green grassy valley with distant low polygon mountains. Keep this recognizable low-poly game visual style, do not make photorealistic soldiers or imply AAA graphics. Create a clean cinematic low-poly promotional illustration with soft sunlight, quiet sky, and a gentle scene perspective. Bold but understated white Swiss-style typography in the left third, exact two-line text "WARLAPSE" and "BATTLE 3D". Small flat white W mark above, consistent with the logo reference. Small quiet text at bottom left exactly "3D BATTLE VIEW • SOLDIER CAMERA" and at bottom right exactly "PROMOTIONAL ART". Design for excellent small-thumbnail readability, visually uncluttered, large empty dark green/charcoal area behind type, generous margins. One tiny muted teal line accent. Right two thirds carries the low-poly valley and tiny formation of soldiers. Flat clean branding only. No shields, crests, gold, swords-as-logos, metallic typography, lens flares, fire, badges, download buttons, fake user interface, extra words or watermarks. Output one coherent finished landscape cover.
```
